Overview

What this journey includes

A combined Ayodhya + Varanasi circuit — Ram Mandir darshan, the Saryu Aarti, and the spiritual depth of Kashi, with comfortable private AC transfers. ₹18,500 ($220) per person. Over 4 days, you will be accompanied by a licensed local guide, chauffeured in an air-conditioned vehicle, and looked after by our dedicated travel team.

VIP Ram Mandir darshan
Saryu River evening Aarti
Hanuman Garhi & Kanak Bhawan
One full day in Varanasi
Reserved Ganga Aarti seating
3 star category hotel with breakfast stay

Day by Day

Your itinerary, hour by hour

A guideline pace — we adjust to your energy, the weather, and the unexpected beauty of the city.

  • Morning pick-up from your Varanasi hotel
  • Drive to Ayodhya (~4–5 hrs)
  • Check-in
  • Evening Saryu Aarti
  • Dinner
  • Pre-dawn darshan booking
  • Ram Janmabhoomi visit
  • Hanuman Garhi
  • Kanak Bhawan
  • Nageshwarnath Temple
  • Ramlila cultural show (evening)
  • Late breakfast
  • Drive back to Varanasi
  • Reserved Dashashwamedh Ganga Aarti
  • Dinner on a rooftop
  • Sunrise boat ride
  • Kashi Vishwanath
  • Drop to airport / station

What's included

  • 3 nights 3 star category hotel with breakfast
  • Private air-conditioned vehicle & driver
  • English-speaking licensed guide
  • Wooden boat ride tickets (Sunrise boat ride)
